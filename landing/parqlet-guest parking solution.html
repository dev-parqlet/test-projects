  • Access private, resident-only system. No public access

  • Verify vehicles through license plate matching

For Residents
Guest parking is a new perk
without a hustle

ParQlet makes it easy for residents to coordinate parking within their building while turning unused parking spots into a valuable resource.

  • Sharing parking spot with neighbors when not using it
  • Booking parking spots for your guests directly within the building
  • Earning credits each time a spot is used
  • Using credits later to book parking for guests or exchange to a gift card

ParQlet introduces a coin-based system that makes sharing your parking spot easy, fun, and rewarding — encouraging residents to make their spots available when not in use.
